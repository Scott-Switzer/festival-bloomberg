# Design Partner Target Profiles

Ideal first design-partner categories, ranked for the live-music use case.
This is **profile research only** — no real contacts are listed, and no one
should be contacted as part of this milestone.

## Ranking dimensions

- **data richness** — how many useful fields a partner of this type typically has
- **repeat-event density** — how much repeat artist/venue/market history they produce
- **decision usefulness** — how valuable their history is for comparable-event research
- **sales difficulty** — how hard it is to establish trust and hand over data
- **competitive sensitivity** — how guarded their economics are
- **likely willingness** — how motivated they are to benchmark themselves
- **commercial learning value** — how much this partner teaches us about real deal structures

## Profiles (ranked)

| # | Profile | richness | repeat density | decision value | sales difficulty | sensitivity | willingness | learning value |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Independent festival operators** | High | High | High | Medium | Medium | High | High |
| 2 | **Regional promoters** | High | High | High | Medium | Medium-High | Medium-High | High |
| 3 | **Independent venue groups** | High | High | Medium-High | Low-Medium | Medium | High | Medium-High |
| 4 | **College concert organizations** | Medium | Medium | Medium | Low | Low | High | Medium |
| 5 | **Boutique talent buyers** | Medium | Medium | High | Low | Medium | High | Medium-High |
| 6 | **Small multi-market promoters** | High | Medium-High | High | Medium-High | High | Medium | High |

## Why this order

1. **Independent festival operators** already aggregate many artists per year in
   one market — the densest artist × edition × slot history per relationship.
2. **Regional promoters** produce recurring artist/venue/market triples across
   years, the ideal comparable-event training corpus.
3. **Venue groups** have the cleanest capacity and attendance data and the least
   competitive sensitivity about individual artist deals.
4. **College organizations** are high-willingness and low-sensitivity, good for
   validating the onboarding flow before approaching more guarded partners.
5. **Boutique buyers** are decision-dense (they pick lineups) but small.
6. **Multi-market promoters** are the highest-value outcome but the most
   competitive-sensitive.

## What we ask for, again

Sanitized historical events: decision dates, capacity, tickets, gross/guarantee,
settlement. **No customer PII.**
